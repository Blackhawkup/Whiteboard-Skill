# Narration

Your car just broke down. So you ask a chatbot what to do. It gives you a list. Call a garage. Book a repair. Find a ride home. Helpful. But you still have to do all of it.

Now, ask an AI agent. It calls the garage, books the repair, orders your ride, and adds it to your calendar. You just go home. A chatbot tells you what to do. An agent does it for you.
