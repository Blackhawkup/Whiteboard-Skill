# Storyboard: Chatbot vs AI agent

## The board
Two rows on one big whiteboard. Top row: the problem and what a chatbot gives you. Bottom row: what an agent does, then home.
Payoff words sit at the right end of each row. The film ends on a pull-back that shows the whole story at once.

| # | Narration | What gets drawn, in order | Why it explains the line | Camera |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your car just broke down. | Car with hood up and smoke, then the red colour | Everyone instantly knows this problem | Close on the car, slow push |
| 2 | So you ask a chatbot what to do. | Worried person. Phone with an empty speech bubble | "You" and "the chatbot" as two real things | One pan right to person + phone |
| 3 | It gives you a list. Call a garage. Book a repair. Find a ride home. | 1, 2, 3 down the bubble, then each task on its word | The answer is words on a screen, nothing has happened yet | Hold, tiny push to read |
| 4 | Helpful. But you still have to do all of it. | CHATBOT label. Three red arrows from the list back to the person. ALL ON YOU. Sweat drops | The work physically lands back on you | Shift left to fit the label |
| 5 | Now, ask an AI agent. | Violet arrow down from the person. Agent phone with a sparkle. AI AGENT label | A second path out of the same person | The camera rides the arrow down |
| 6 | It calls the garage, books the repair, orders your ride, and adds it to your calendar. | One line out of the agent and one drawing per job, each on its verb. Four ticks | The same list as before, now done by something else | Hold, slow push |
| 7 | You just go home. | House. Same person relaxed in an armchair | The payoff of the same character | Pan right |
| 8 | A chatbot tells you what to do. An agent does it for you. | TELLS. beside the chatbot row. DOES. beside the agent row, underlined | The whole idea in two words, next to its proof | Pull back to the whole board |
