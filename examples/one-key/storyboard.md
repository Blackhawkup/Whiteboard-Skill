# Storyboard: One key, every door
For: everyday, non-technical people. They should come away with: why one password everywhere is risky, and wanting a password manager.

## The board
Two stacked clusters, the same story told twice, on one tall board. Top: one key, three doors, thief opens them all. Below: a safe, a matching key per door, thief gets the gym only.
Right-hand column stays empty until the end, when the camera pulls back to show both clusters beside two huge payoff words.

| # | Narration | What gets drawn, in order | Why it explains the line | Camera |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One key opens your house, your car and your bank. | Big key, coral handle. House, car, bank in a row, an arrow from the key to each on its word | One thing physically reaching everything you own | Close on the key, pull back to the row |
| 2 | You leave a copy at the gym. | Small copy of the same key, then the gym | The copy sits somewhere else, out of your hands | Down to the gym |
| 3 | It gets robbed. | Coral burst lines on the gym | The robbery is the impact, not a caption | Hold |
| 4 | A thief takes your key. | Thief holding the coral key, short arrow from the copy to his hand | Same coral key, now his | Slide right to the thief |
| 5 | He tries your house. Open. Your car. Open. Your bank. Open. | Coral arrow up to each door on its noun, OPEN under each door on the word | The whole row falls, one at a time. Signature beat | Pull back to the whole cluster |
| 6 | That's one password everywhere. | ONE PASSWORD above the key | The analogy clicks: the key was the password | Hold, slow push |
| 7 | A password manager is a safe. | Violet arrow down, safe with a dial, PASSWORD MANAGER above it | The product named as a thing you can picture | Ride the arrow down to the safe |

| 11 | Not every door. | Pull-back. ONE DOOR. beside the lower cluster, EVERY DOOR. beside the upper | Both stories side by side, the whole idea in two phrases | The whole board |

## Drawings to generate (10)
key: big door key, coral handle
house: small house, cyan roof
car: small car facing right, amber body
bank: bank with columns, green roof
gym: gym building with a dumbbell sign, no colour
thief: burglar with an eye mask, holding up a key, coral key
safe: big safe with a round dial, violet door
key_round: key with a round handle, cyan
key_square: key with a square handle, amber
key_star: key with a star handle, green
(key_copy reuses the key image at a smaller size; house, car, bank, gym and thief are drawn twice from one image.)

Colour roles: coral = the problem, violet = the solution, cyan / amber / green = which door goes with which key.
