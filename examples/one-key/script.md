# Narration

One key opens your house, your car and your bank. You leave a copy at the gym. It gets robbed. A thief takes your key. He tries your house. Open. Your car. Open. Your bank. Open.

That's one password everywhere. A password manager is a safe. It makes every door its own key, and keeps them inside. You just remember one code. Now the thief gets one door. Not every door.
